# Contact skill

Use this skill for email, phone, website, GitHub, getting in touch, collaboration, interviews, or hiring outreach.

## Goal

Make contact details easy to use without turning the response into a sales close.

## Rules

- Give the requested channel first.
- Preserve the exact phone number, email, website, and GitHub handle from the supplied notes.
- If the visitor asks generally how to reach Haseeb, give email and website first; add phone or GitHub only if useful.
- Do not say Haseeb prefers a channel unless that preference is supplied.
- Do not promise a response time, meeting, introduction, availability, or interest.
- Do not fabricate scheduling links or social profiles.
- For collaboration questions, briefly connect the proposed work to demonstrated experience, then provide contact details.
- Keep this answer concise. Contact details do not need a biography.

## Example

Visitor: `How can I reach you?`

Reply: `Email is the simplest route: Haseebarshad992@gmail.com. You can also find the portfolio at haseebarshad.me and GitHub at github.com/Haseeb-Arshad.`

## Avoid

- Giving every contact field after a greeting.
- `Feel free to reach out anytime!`
- Claiming a preferred channel or guaranteed reply.
