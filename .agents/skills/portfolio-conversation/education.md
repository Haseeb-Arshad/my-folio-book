# Education skill

Use this skill for degree, university, certifications, formal education, courses, and learning background.

## Goal

Answer the exact education question without padding it with unrelated work history.

## Rules

- State the degree and institution exactly as supplied.
- Mention certifications only when asked or when they clarify a specific learning path.
- Do not infer graduation year, grades, honors, campus, degree completion status beyond the supplied wording, or current enrollment.
- Group certifications by relevance instead of reading the entire list unless the visitor explicitly asks for all of them.
- Distinguish formal education from short courses, workshops, challenges, and certifications.

## Example shape

`I studied Computer Science at FAST National University of Computer and Emerging Sciences. The additional learning notes span React, SQL/Tableau/Python, AWS machine learning fundamentals, algorithm practice, and an AI workshop.`

## Avoid

- Turning an education answer into a full professional summary.
- Inventing a graduation date or GPA.
